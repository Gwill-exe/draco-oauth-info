Privacy policy

This policy covers the personal-use Google integration named hermes-email-bot, used by the Draco assistant.

Access and use

With the owner's consent, the integration can access Gmail messages and associated metadata, send messages, change message labels and organization, and access and manage Google Calendar calendars and events. Only authorized functions are used to fulfill the owner's requests. Calendar data may include event descriptions, times, locations, and attendee details; email data may include senders, recipients, subjects, bodies, and attachments when accessed for a requested task.

Processing and sharing

The assistant runs on infrastructure controlled by its owner. Information used in an assistant request may be sent to the configured external AI model provider for processing. Message sending, invitations, and other owner-authorized actions may transmit information to intended recipients through Google. Google data is not sold or used for advertising. It is not intentionally supplied for training generalized AI models. Provider-side processing and retention are also governed by the selected provider's terms and account settings; this policy does not promise exclusively local processing or zero retention.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored separately on the owner's infrastructure and are not published in this website or its repository. Conversation history, local logs, caches, and backups may retain information processed by the assistant. Retention depends on the owner's configuration and cleanup practices; no fixed deletion period is promised here. Access to the assistant and its credentials must be restricted by the owner.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke the integration in Google Account third-party connections at any time. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not delete existing local transcripts, caches, or backups. Those must be removed separately, together with stored credentials when the integration is retired.

Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, use the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen. This is a private personal-use integration, not a public account service. This policy must be updated if the integration's data practices change.
